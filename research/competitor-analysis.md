# Amatchless competitor analysis: high-traffic, high-authority agencies

Prepared 30 Oct 2026 for the Amatchless blog/SEO project. Brief: find competitors in the same combined business (software + AI + marketing) with high website traffic and high domain authority, and learn what their blogs do well.

## Read this first: what the numbers are

- **No paid SEO tool was used.** All numbers come from the free public pages of Ahrefs (`ahrefs.com/websites/<domain>`), Semrush (`semrush.com/website/<domain>/overview/`) and Similarweb (`similarweb.com/website/<domain>/`), read on 30 Oct 2026. They are **estimates**, and each tool uses its own scale.
- **Moz DA/PA could not be pulled.** Moz shows DA/PA only after a sign-in. The closest public equivalents are used: **Ahrefs DR** (0-100) and **Semrush Authority Score** (0-100). They are not the same as Moz DA (Semrush scores run noticeably lower than Ahrefs DR for the same site, e.g. WebFX: DR 88 vs AS 52). How to get real DA/PA for free is at the end.
- **Many public pages blocked automated reads**, so some cells are blank. Where only a **Similarweb global rank** was available, use it as a traffic proxy: a smaller number means more traffic (rank #80,000 gets far more visits than #2,000,000).
- **Service overlap** is based on each company's public positioning as generally known; confirm on their sites before quoting it anywhere.

### Patterns to adopt on amatchless.com/blog

1. **Cost and pricing posts in INR and USD.** Every high-traffic competitor ranks for "how much does X cost" (Google Ads, app development, SEO, websites). Amatchless can own India-specific versions: "Google Ads cost in India 2026", "Cost to build an e-commerce website in India", "Shopify store cost for creators".
2. **Named, credentialed author on every post**, with bio and photo. All five do this; Amatchless currently posts as "Amatchless Team". The founder byline (like Single Grain) is the strongest E-E-A-T signal.
3. **Standard post template:** key takeaways box, table of contents, FAQ section (5-10 Qs, with FAQ schema), updated date, one embedded video from the Amatchless YouTube channel.
4. **Industry and location angles:** "SEO for salons", "Meta ads for fashion D2C brands", "digital marketing agency in Rohini/Delhi". Pair with service and location pages (currently missing, see the brief).
5. **Own data and tools.** A simple ROAS / ad break-even calculator or automation-hours-saved calculator on a post earns links the way WebFX's CPC calculator does. Case-study numbers (Bailey's Blossoms 4x ROAS) belong in-line.
6. **AI search / GEO topics now.** Neil Patel, WebFX and Single Grain are all publishing on AI Overviews, GEO and AI visibility. Amatchless already sells AEO/GEO, so this is an early, credible lane.
7. **Every post links to one service page and one case study**, and ends with a discovery-call or free-audit CTA (WebFX's "free audit" and Thrive's "free proposal" are the models).
8. **Volume and freshness.** Appinventiv publishes daily and WebFX has 160+ pages of posts. Amatchless should aim for a steady weekly cadence and refresh "2026" posts each year.

## How to get real Moz DA/PA (free, about 10 minutes)

1. Create a free account at moz.com and open **Link Explorer** (free tier allows a limited number of queries per month), or install the free **MozBar** Chrome extension.
2. Enter each domain from the table above and note **Domain Authority**. Page Authority (PA) is per page, so check it on each competitor's blog URL or on the specific ranking post you want to beat.
3. Add amatchless.com for a baseline. Expect a low single/double-digit DA for a new site; the gap to these competitors is what the blog and link-building need to close.
