# Competitor keywords in India (for Amatchless blog and service pages)

Prepared 30 Oct 2026. Companion to [competitor-analysis.md](competitor-analysis.md). Use this list when picking blog topics and building service/location pages.

## Read this first: how reliable this is

- **No India rank or search-volume data could be pulled for free.** Semrush and Ahrefs only show "top keywords" to signed-in users or rate-limited bots; the one table that loaded (Thrive, Semrush) was Bangladesh data, not India. So **no search volumes are given in this file.**
- The keywords below come from three kinds of evidence, labelled in every table:
  - **[R] Ranks (self-reported):** the competitor publishes these on its own site as keywords it ranks for in Google (PageTraffic's "Here's How PageTraffic Ranks in Google" table). Not independently verified.
  - **[T] Targets:** the keyword is the title, H1 or URL of a competitor's service, location or industry page. Competitors build these pages to rank for these terms, so treat them as the keywords they compete on, not proof of rank.
  - **[B] Blog:** the keyword is the main topic of a recent competitor blog post.
- Sources: homepages, service menus, sitemaps and blogs of Techmagnate, PageTraffic, Infidigit, Appinventiv, ValueCoders and SparxIT, read 30 Oct 2026. Infidigit's city pages came from a summary of its menu and were not opened one by one.
- **To get real India volumes for free:** paste the keywords into **Google Keyword Planner** (Google Ads > Tools > Keyword Planner > "Get search volume and forecasts", location: India). Amatchless already runs Google Ads, so the account exists. Google Search Console on amatchless.com will show which of these terms you start appearing for.
