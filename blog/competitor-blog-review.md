# Competitor blog review: what the 15 competitors are publishing

Read 30 Oct 2026 from each competitor's blog index (and the latest posts where possible). Companion to [../research/competitor-analysis.md](../research/competitor-analysis.md).

## Summary

- **The big US agencies (WebFX, Neil Patel, Thrive, SmartSites, Ignite, Single Grain) are all-in on AI search** (AI Overviews, AEO/GEO, ChatGPT visibility) plus vertical/local SEO and Google reviews. They publish several times a week with named authors.
- **The Indian software firms (Appinventiv, ValueCoders, SparxIT, OpenXcell) write for enterprises**: AI cost, AI platforms, legacy modernisation, AI coding tools, and geo pages (Appinventiv targets Australia). Prices, when given, are in USD and start at enterprise budgets.
- **Nobody among the 15 writes practical AI/automation content for Indian small businesses**, in rupees, with WhatsApp, GST, UPI and DPDP. That gap is the first post's topic.
- Techmagnate (the closest Delhi marketing rival) has an effectively dormant blog (newest listed post Jan 2021). Uplers' content is all hiring. Major Tom and Coalition are brand/web/e-commerce focused.

## Topics that are clearly crowded (avoid head-on for now)

- "How much does Google Ads cost" (WebFX, plus 10+ Indian agencies on page 1 for the India version).
- Generic AI SEO / GEO explainers (Neil Patel, WebFX, Thrive, Coalition, Single Grain all have one).
- Enterprise AI cost and readiness (SparxIT, ValueCoders, Appinventiv).

## Open lanes for Amatchless

1. **AI and automation for Indian small businesses** (rupees, WhatsApp, GST, UPI, DPDP). Chosen for week 1.
2. **Influencer marketing in India** for D2C brands and creators (none of the 15 cover it).
3. **Branding for small businesses and creator brands** (none of the India competitors cover it).
4. **Meta/Instagram ads for D2C in India**, with ROAS maths (thin coverage; Single Grain's Meta-cost post is US-centric).
5. **Local: Rohini / North and West Delhi** service and SEO posts (no competitor has a page).
6. **Google reviews and local SEO for Indian shops, clinics and salons** (Thrive owns this in the US; nobody does it for India).

## Patterns worth copying (now applied to the week-1 post)

Named author byline and bio, key takeaways box, table of contents, comparison tables, an FAQ block with FAQ schema, a sources list, in-line case-study links, and one clear discovery-call CTA.
