## What's ranking now (19 Nov 2026), and how our post beats it

| Page | Type | Gaps |
|---|---|---|
| upgrowth.in (several posts: improve ROAS D2C India, pricing models 2026) | Indian growth agency | Good data, but spread across many posts; no break-even table with GST; no case study |
| imarkinfotech.com "Indian D2C brands are getting destroyed on Meta ads in 2026" | Agency | ~2,800 words, lots of percentages with no sources or sample; no calculator or worked example |
| sociolabs.in "How D2C brands in India can achieve 3x–4x ROAS" | Agency | Claims 3.2x average without methodology; no budget maths |
| balistro.com (D2C performance marketing guides) | Agency | Category overviews, little Meta-specific maths |
| noirandblanco.com (Meta ads packages / agency India) | Agency sales pages | Pricing of the agency, not of ads |
| echai.ventures D2C Meta ads fundamentals | Aggregator | Thin summaries of other posts |
| adynox pages.dev, vercel.app agency blogs | Weak domains | Short, generic |

**Our post (~4,300 words incl. tables) wins on:**
1. Break-even ROAS table by margin, **with and without GST on ad spend**, plus a worked ₹1,180 example that shows the GST-inclusive purchase value trap. None of the ranking pages do this.
2. Test budget derived from Meta's learning phase (about 50 events a week), not a random number.
3. Cost ranges with dates and sources (upGrowth Sept 2026, Superads benchmark), and GST explained from Meta's own help page.
4. Reported ROAS vs blended MER table, with COD/RTO handled.
5. Conversions API from Meta's developer docs (redundant setup, deduplication, Event Match Quality).
6. Real case study (Bailey's Blossoms, 4x ROAS), only claims shown on amatchless.com/work/.
7. 5 on-brand graphics, a crop-safe unique cover, 8 FAQs, clickable sources.

Research note: this run could reach the web directly. Meta Business Help Centre pages render with JavaScript and could not be read in full from the cloud; the learning phase and GST facts are as described in Meta's help pages and confirmed by several secondary sources. Triple Whale blocks automated reads; its ~1.9 median Meta ROAS comes from its page as reported in search results. Worth a quick look by a human if time allows.
