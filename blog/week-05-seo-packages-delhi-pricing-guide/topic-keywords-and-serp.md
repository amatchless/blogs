## What's ranking now (26 Nov 2026), and how our post beats it

| Page | Type | Gaps |
|---|---|---|
| RankingBySEO, affordable SEO packages | Agency sales page | Redirects/plans only; prices in USD on homepage FAQ; no explanation of tiers |
| Infidigit, SEO plans | Agency sales page | No visible prices or inclusions; menus and badges |
| ZestRank, "SEO packages and pricing in Delhi" (updated 21 Nov 2026) | Agency blog | Good tier prices (₹10k-20k / 20k-50k / 50k-1L+, excl. GST) but no inclusions by tier, no quote checklist |
| Beyond DigitA, "SEO services cost in Delhi" (27 Sep 2026) | Agency blog | Best price data (by business and provider type); no performance-model discussion, no red-flag sources |
| Battersea Web Expert, "Is cheap SEO worth it in Delhi?" | Agency blog | Risks listed without citing Google |
| IndiaMART listings (₹8,000/month packs) | Marketplace | No scope detail |
| upGrowth SEO and PPC cost calculator | Tool | Not Delhi-specific |

**Our post wins on:**
1. One side-by-side tier table (price, fit, technical, pages, content, local, links, AI answers, reporting).
2. Pricing models compared, including performance-linked deals and their risks.
3. Prices by business type (local shop, B2B, D2C) and by provider type, all sourced and dated.
4. Red flags backed by Google's own words ("No one can guarantee a #1 ranking"; link spam definition).
5. A 10-question quote checklist (two questions straight from Google) plus a "rupees per deliverable" method.
6. 5 graphics, a unique cover, 7 FAQs, clickable sources.

Research note: ZestRank, Beyond DigitA, Battersea and both Google pages were read in full on 26 Nov 2026. Google's "Do you need an SEO?" page no longer gives a time frame in months, so the post gives none.
