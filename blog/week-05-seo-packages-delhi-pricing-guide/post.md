# SEO Packages in Delhi: What Each Price Tier Includes and What to Ask Before You Pay

*By Amatchless Team · Rohini, New Delhi · Updated 26 November 2026 · 10 min read*

Search for SEO packages in Delhi and you will find offers from ₹2,000 a month to well over ₹1 lakh. Both ends of that range can be honest. They are simply buying very different amounts of work.

This guide explains what each price tier usually includes, how agencies charge, where affordable turns into risky, and how to compare two quotes like for like. Every market price below is a range taken from published 2026 agency pages, linked in the sources, so you can check them yourself.

> **Key takeaways**
>
> - **Most SEO packages in Delhi cost about ₹10,000 to ₹1,00,000+ a month** in 2026, according to published agency price guides. Entry plans sit near ₹10,000 to ₹20,000, growth plans near ₹20,000 to ₹50,000, and advanced or e-commerce plans from about ₹50,000.
> - **Price follows hours of skilled work**: how many pages get fixed, how much content gets written, and whether anyone earns real links.
> - **Below about ₹5,000 a month, expect very little work**, and watch for bulk backlinks, which Google treats as link spam.
> - **Nobody can guarantee a #1 ranking.** Google says so in its own advice on hiring an SEO.
> - **Compare quotes on deliverables, access and reporting**, not on the monthly number.

## What do SEO packages in Delhi typically include?

**Most SEO packages in Delhi bundle five kinds of work: a technical audit and fixes, on-page optimisation of key pages, local SEO (Google Business Profile, citations, reviews), new content, and monthly reporting. Higher tiers add link building, more content, e-commerce or multi-location work, and optimisation for AI answers.**

In practice, the line items on a quote fall into these buckets:

- **Technical SEO:** site speed, mobile issues, broken pages, indexing and crawl problems, schema markup.
- **On-page SEO:** keyword research, page titles, headings, internal links and copy for your service or product pages.
- **Local SEO:** Google Business Profile setup and posts, local citations, location pages and a plan for reviews (our guide on [how to get more Google reviews](https://amatchless.com/how-to-get-more-google-reviews/) covers that part).
- **Content:** new blog posts, guides or landing pages aimed at searches your customers make.
- **Off-page:** earning mentions and links from relevant sites, not buying them in bulk.
- **Reporting:** a monthly report built on Google Search Console and Analytics data you can see yourself.

The tier you pay for decides how much of each bucket you actually get.

## How much do SEO services cost in Delhi? Pricing models compared

SEO charges in Delhi come in three main shapes. Most small and mid-sized businesses buy a monthly retainer.

![SEO pricing models in Delhi: monthly retainer, one-time project and performance-linked deals compared](seo-pricing-models-delhi.webp)

| Pricing model | How it works | Typical SEO price in Delhi (2026) | Best for | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly retainer | A fixed fee for an agreed set of work each month | ₹10,000 to ₹1,00,000+ a month | Ongoing growth | Vague scopes ("SEO work") with no list of deliverables |
| One-time project | A fixed fee for a defined job, such as an audit or a site migration | Audit: about ₹5,000 to ₹40,000 one-time | A health check before you commit | An audit no one acts on is wasted |

Retainer and audit ranges come from ZestRank and Beyond DigitA (sources 1 and 2). ZestRank quotes its figures excluding GST.

Who does the work also moves the price. Beyond DigitA's 2026 guide puts freelancers at about **₹8,000 to ₹25,000** a month, small agencies at **₹25,000 to ₹60,000**, and large agencies at **₹1,00,000+**, while an in-house SEO hire costs roughly **₹40,000 to ₹80,000** a month in salary.

## Affordable vs cheap SEO in Delhi: where the risks start

Affordable SEO services in Delhi and cheap SEO services in Delhi are not the same thing. Affordable means the price fits your stage and you still get real, visible work. Cheap means the price is so low that real work can't fit into it.

The maths is simple. A ₹3,000 package doesn't pay for many hours of a skilled person's time, so something has to give. Usually it is replaced by automated reports, copied content or bulk links. ZestRank calls packages at ₹2,000 to ₹5,000 a month a red flag, and Beyond DigitA links offers of ₹5,000 or less with low-quality tactics.

Low cost SEO in Delhi can still make sense for a small shop that needs its Google Business Profile set up and a few pages fixed. Just buy it as a small, defined job, not as "complete SEO".

<a id="red-flags"></a>
### Red flags: guaranteed rankings, bulk backlinks, no reporting access

![Cheap SEO red flags in Delhi: guaranteed rankings, bulk backlinks, no access to your own data, secret methods and long lock-ins](cheap-seo-red-flags-delhi.webp)

- **Guaranteed rankings.** Google's own guide to hiring an SEO says: "No one can guarantee a #1 ranking on Google." If someone guarantees first place, find someone else.
- **Bulk backlinks.** "500 backlinks a month" usually means links built for rankings, not for people. Google's spam policies define link spam as links created "primarily for the purpose of manipulating search rankings", including paying for links. That can lower your rankings instead of raising them.
- **No access to your own data.** You should own your Search Console, Analytics and Google Business Profile, and give the agency access, not the other way round.
- **Secret methods.** Google warns against SEOs who are secretive or "won't clearly explain what they intend to do."
- **Reports with no source.** A PDF of rankings for keywords nobody searches is not proof of progress.
- **Long lock-ins with no exit.** A 12-month contract you can't leave, with no deliverables list, protects the agency, not you.

## How to compare two SEO quotes like for like

Put both quotes side by side and ask the same ten questions. If one agency can't answer them in writing, that is your answer.

![Checklist to compare two SEO quotes in Delhi: deliverables, people, access, links, reporting and exit terms](compare-seo-quotes-checklist.webp)

1. **What exactly do I get each month?** Number of pages optimised, articles written, technical fixes, and local SEO tasks.
2. **Who does the work?** A named senior person, a junior team, or an outsourced partner?
3. **What did you find in my site already?** A good agency looks at your site before quoting.
4. **Which keywords, and why?** They should be searches your buyers make, not vanity terms.
5. **How will you get links?** Ask for examples. "We have a network" is a warning sign.
6. **Do you follow Google Search Essentials?** One of the questions Google itself suggests asking.
7. **What results do you expect, and in what timeframe?** Also from Google's list. Expect a range, not a promise.
8. **Do I own all the accounts and content?** Search Console, Analytics, Business Profile, and every article you pay for.
9. **What does the monthly report show?** Clicks, enquiries and sales from search, not just rankings.
10. **How do I leave?** Notice period, and what you keep if you go.

Then convert both quotes into the same unit: **rupees per deliverable**. A ₹20,000 plan with four articles and real outreach can be better value than a ₹12,000 plan with an automated report.

## FAQs

**How much do SEO packages in Delhi cost?**
Published 2026 agency guides put most SEO packages in Delhi between about ₹10,000 and ₹1,00,000+ a month. Entry plans start near ₹10,000 to ₹20,000, growth plans run about ₹20,000 to ₹50,000, and advanced or e-commerce SEO usually costs ₹50,000 or more.

**What is a sensible monthly SEO budget for a small business in Delhi?**
For a single-location business, published Delhi guides put local SEO at about ₹15,000 to ₹30,000 a month. Below about ₹15,000, expect upkeep rather than growth. Start with a one-time audit (about ₹5,000 to ₹40,000) if you aren't sure what your site needs.

**Is cheap SEO worth it?**
Usually not as a complete package. At ₹5,000 a month or less there is little room for skilled work, and some cheap plans rely on bulk links that Google treats as spam. A small, clearly defined job, such as setting up your Google Business Profile, can be good value.

**How do I find affordable SEO services in Delhi without the risks?**
Ask for a written list of monthly deliverables, keep ownership of your Search Console and Business Profile, and check the agency's case studies. A fair SEO price in Delhi buys visible work you can check each month.

**How long does SEO take to show results?**
Google doesn't give a timeline and suggests asking any SEO what results to expect and in what timeframe. Local and long-tail searches tend to move first; competitive keywords take longer. Be wary of anyone who promises fixed rankings by a fixed date.

**Can an SEO agency guarantee first-page rankings?**
No. Google says no one can guarantee a #1 ranking on Google. An agency can commit to the work it will do and the reporting you will get.

**What are SEO charges in Delhi for a one-time audit?**
Published 2026 ranges run from about ₹5,000 to ₹40,000, depending on the size of the site and how deep the audit goes.
