> **Weeks 5 to 9 replaced on 19 Nov 2026.** The owner adopted their own strategy sheet in sheet order: see [content-calendar-2026-11-27-strategy-sheet.md](content-calendar-2026-11-27-strategy-sheet.md). Week 4 (Meta Ads, Fri 20 Nov) is the last post from this calendar. The week 5 to 9 briefs below are kept only as backlog ideas, not scheduled.

Prepared 10 Nov 2026. One post a week, published on the Friday of each week (changed from Tuesday on 18 Nov 2026). Goal: organic traffic to amatchless.com that turns into discovery calls. Built from the competitor research in `research/` and `blog/competitor-blog-review.md`, plus a fresh check of what ranks on Google today for each target keyword (10 Nov 2026, notes below each post).

**About the keywords.** No free tool gives India search volumes, so none are quoted here. Before writing each post, paste its primary and secondary keywords into Google Keyword Planner (location: India) and swap a secondary in or out if it has no volume. Search Console will show which terms each post starts to appear for after 3 to 6 weeks.

**About "why it can rank".** Amatchless is a young domain, so every pick avoids SERPs held by high-authority sites (Hostinger, WebFX, HubSpot-level). Most page-1 results below are small agency blogs, vendor sales pages, dev.to, blogspot and pages.dev sites. Those are beatable with a deeper, better-sourced, India-specific post.

## Week 3 · 17 Nov · Google reviews and Google Maps ranking

- **Working title:** How to Get More Google Reviews in India (and Rank Higher on Google Maps)
- **Slug:** `/how-to-get-more-google-reviews/`
- **Primary keyword:** how to get more google reviews
- **Secondary:** how to get google reviews in india · google business profile reviews · how to rank higher on google maps · google maps ranking india · how to reply to google reviews · google review qr code · fake / negative google reviews
- **Intent:** Informational, local business owners (shops, clinics, salons, restaurants, coaching centres). Searchers want a method they can apply this week.
- **Service promoted:** SEO, local/maps (Google Business Profile setup and management). Case study: /work/aleuco/ (local/maps).
- **Why it can rank:** Page 1 is thin: junoschool.pages.dev, meshworld.in, gonukkad.com, a dance-academy forum thread, an old WordStream post from 2020. Thrive owns this topic in the US (a whole reviews series) and nobody does the India version with WhatsApp review requests, Hindi/English message templates and counter QR standees. Can include reply templates and a "what Google's policy forbids" section, which none of the ranking pages explain properly.
- **Angle / must include:** step-by-step review link from Google Business Profile, WhatsApp and SMS ask templates, timing, QR standee, reply templates (positive, negative, fake), what is banned (incentives, gating), how reviews affect Maps rank alongside category, NAP and proximity, 30-day review plan.
- **Internal links:** /services/, /work/aleuco/, week 1 (automating review requests on WhatsApp).

## Week 4 · Fri 20 Nov · Meta ads for D2C in India

- **Working title:** Meta Ads for D2C Brands in India: Budgets, Break-even ROAS and What 4x Looks Like
- **Slug:** `/meta-ads-for-d2c-brands-india/`
- **Primary keyword:** meta ads for d2c brands india
- **Secondary:** facebook ads cost in india · instagram ads cost india · meta ads budget india · good roas for d2c india · break-even roas · meta ads agency india · how to improve roas
- **Intent:** Commercial investigation. Founders deciding how much to spend or whether to hire an agency.
- **Service promoted:** Digital Marketing & Ads. Case study: /work/baileys-blossoms/ (4x ROAS on Meta). Supporting post: /first-profitable-ad/.
- **Why it can rank:** Page 1 is small Indian agencies (upGrowth, Sociolabs, Balistro, iMark Infotech, Clicksbazaar) with conflicting ROAS numbers (1.8x to 6x) and little maths. The India competitors in our research barely cover Meta ads. Amatchless has a real 4x ROAS case study and an existing break-even post to build on. Timely: brands are planning December sale and wedding-season budgets.
- **Angle / must include:** minimum test budgets in rupees, CPM/CPC ranges with 18% GST, break-even ROAS calculator table by gross margin, reported ROAS vs blended MER, Conversions API, creative testing plan, when to hire an agency, 4x case study walkthrough.
- **Internal links:** /services/, /work/baileys-blossoms/, /first-profitable-ad/, week 2 (influencer + ads).
