## Strengths

- **Real search volumes.** The current calendar has none. Website cluster terms are the strongest: responsive web design 6,600, website development cost in india 5,000, website making cost 5,000, website design and development 4,400.
- **Production-ready briefs.** Full heading outlines with a 40-60 word direct answer under the first H2 (good for AI Overviews / featured snippets), slugs, schema and internal links. This makes each post faster and more consistent.
- **Strong buyer intent and local fit.** Delhi / NCR targeting matches the Rohini base. Cost and "how to choose" posts attract people close to hiring.
- **Tight internal linking.** Posts link to each other inside two clear clusters, plus /services/, case studies and /contact/.
- **Honest notes.** No ranking guarantees, correct note that FAQ schema no longer earns rich results, flags for compliance and pricing.

## Gaps and risks

1. **Half the business is missing.** No post for AI Solutions, Process Automation, Influencer Marketing, Meta / Google Ads, or Branding. That is "Software × AI" plus two growth services. The sheet's own note 14 says so. 10 of 15 posts are SEO or website.
2. **No AEO / GEO / LLMO post, despite the title.** The current calendar's "How to show up in ChatGPT and AI Overviews" (week 5) is the post that would prove the claim. Suggest keeping it.
3. **SEO posts 1, 2, 4 and 5 will compete with each other and with a service page.** All four target "I want to hire SEO" queries (seo packages / consultant / expert services / marketing agency in Delhi). Google usually shows agency service pages, not blog posts, for these. The sheet's own note 10 says head terms belong on service pages, and Amatchless has no SEO service page yet. Suggest: build one SEO service page for these head terms, keep post 1 (packages / pricing) and post 2 (consultant vs agency) as blogs, and merge 4 into 5 or drop it.
4. **Post 8 targets a 100K listicle query** ("digital advertising companies in india"). Very unlikely to rank. Retarget to something like "how to choose a performance marketing agency".
5. **Post 9 (doctors) needs a medical / legal reviewer**, which clashes with Friday publishing without review. Hold it until a reviewer is named, or rewrite it as a general clinic / local-services guide.
6. **Cost posts (1, 6, 10) need your real price ranges.** Without them they are another list of market ranges. The current calendar already asks for this for the website cost post.
7. **"Affordable / cheap SEO" keywords** pull a price-shopping audience and sit oddly with the senior, one-partner positioning. Use them lightly inside a "cheap vs good" section, as the outline already does, and do not put them in titles.
8. **Keyword counts** ("9 times") are fine as a guide, but the writer should not force them.
9. **Seasonal posts dropped.** "Digital marketing trends 2027" and "Marketing budget 2027" are timed for the December to January search spike. Worth keeping in December.
10. **Small fixes.** Local SEO Delhi (3) overlaps the live Google reviews post; add a link to `/how-to-get-more-google-reviews/` and keep 3 focused on Maps ranking factors and citations. No post links to the reviews post at all. Slugs of existing posts in the sheet are correct.
