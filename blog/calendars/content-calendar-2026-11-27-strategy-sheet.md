## Rules that apply to every post from this sheet

- **Follow the outline** (H1/H2/H3) from the brief. Wording can be tightened, but keep the structure and the 40-60 word direct answer under the first H2.
- **Keyword counts are a guide**, not a quota. Never force unnatural repetition.
- **Word count** is the target length (±10%).
- **Schema:** BlogPosting + BreadcrumbList + FAQPage, author as a Person (FAQPage is for machine readability; it won't earn a Google rich result).
- **Internal links:** link only to pages that are live on the day. Links in a brief to a blog post that is not published yet are skipped, and added to the older post when the later post goes live (the Friday run does this). Links to /services/, /work/..., /ai-automation/, /#process and /contact/ are live.
- **Pricing:** cost and package posts (1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 7, 10, 12, 13) use [../pricing-amatchless-and-delhi-market.md](../pricing-amatchless-and-delhi-market.md): the owner's Amatchless prices (SEO / digital marketing from ₹25,000 a month; Meta ads 15% of budget or ₹5,000, whichever is higher; websites from ₹15,000) plus the Delhi market ranges with sources. Re-check market figures on live pages and never invent prices.
- **Post 9 (doctors):** the sheet says it should be reviewed by a healthcare advertising / legal reviewer. Ask the owner in the week before (by Mon 18 Jan 2027) whether a reviewer is confirmed. If not, write it compliance-aware (no outcome claims, no patient testimonials, follow NMC professional conduct rules on advertising) and cite the rules.
- **Post 8:** the primary keyword is a 100K listicle query. Keep the title, but check the live SERP before writing and lean on the ROAS scorecard angle.
- Client results only as already shown on amatchless.com/work/; client metrics need client approval.
- Everything else per `blog/blog-style-guide.md` (unique cover, graphics, sources, Yoast fields).
