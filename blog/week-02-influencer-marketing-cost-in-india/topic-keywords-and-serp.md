## Topic

**Influencer marketing in India: what creators charge in 2026 and how to run a campaign that pays back.**

Why this one now (week 2 in [topic-backlog.md](../topic-backlog.md)):
- **Open lane among competitors.** None of the 15 competitors in `research/` covers influencer marketing, and Amatchless has no dedicated page for the service, so this post becomes the page Google can rank for it.
- **Commercial intent.** People searching costs are brands about to spend, which suits the discovery-call CTA.
- **Proof to point to.** Isola (creator brand, 575K followers) and Bailey's Blossoms (4x ROAS on Meta) support the "creators + ads" angle.
- **The SERP is crowded but samey.** The ranking pages are almost all vendor rate cards (see below). None does the break-even maths, the full hidden-cost stack, or the ASCI / CCPA / TDS rules in one place.

**Our post (~3,600 words) wins on:**
1. **The real cost, not just the fee:** a full hidden-cost table and graphic (usage rights, exclusivity, GST, management, product).
2. **Break-even maths:** a step-by-step worked example showing why posts alone lose money and how ads and repeat orders make it pay back. No ranking page does this.
3. **Rules in one place:** ASCI labels and timings, the health/finance addendum, CCPA penalties, TDS (194R moving to section 393 from 1 April 2026), GST and DPDP.
4. **Budget planner** across four budget levels, and a format-ratio table that stays useful when prices change.
5. **Sourced market data** (EY, Kofluence, ASCI's FY26 report) and credits to every rate card used.
6. Five on-brand graphics plus a crop-safe cover; key takeaways, TOC, 8 FAQs, clickable sources.
