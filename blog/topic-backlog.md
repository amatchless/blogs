## Rules for every post

- Read the top 5 ranking pages before writing; beat them on depth, current data and India specifics.
- Named author byline and bio; key takeaways; TOC; tables; 6-10 FAQs with FAQ schema; sources list.
- 2+ internal links to service/case-study pages and 1+ to an earlier post; add a link *to* the new post from an older page.
- Real meta title (≤60 chars) and meta description (≤155 chars).
- Refresh any "2026" post in January with new numbers and change the year.
