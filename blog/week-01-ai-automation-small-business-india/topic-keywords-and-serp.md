## Topic

**AI automation for small businesses in India: what to automate, what it costs, how to start (2026).**

Why this one first:
- **Weakest search results of the candidates checked.** Google's current results for the keyword are mostly a dev.to post, two `pages.dev` / `github.io` sites, a 2023 government portal article, a business school blog, and small agency posts (detail below). There is no strong, current, rupee-priced guide.
- **None of the 15 competitors cover it for small businesses.** SparxIT, ValueCoders and Appinventiv write about AI for enterprises in USD.
- **Matches Amatchless's strongest page.** It links naturally to /ai-automation/ (the only dedicated service page apart from /services/) and to the existing post /marketing-tasks-to-automate/.
- **Commercial intent.** People searching it are owners looking for help, which suits the discovery-call CTA.

Candidates also checked and parked: "google ads cost in india 2026" (crowded: upgrowth, megadigital, themediaant and 10+ agencies), "influencer marketing cost in india 2026" (moderately crowded: upgrowth, starclinch, grynow, tring; good for a later week).

**Our post (~3,700 words incl. sources) wins on:**
1. **Rupee costs at three levels**, plus a real WhatsApp message-cost table and worked example. Only Nexolve has any INR figures, and nobody prices WhatsApp messages.
2. **Sourced Indian data** (Udyam 7.83 crore, LinkedIn/YouGov, PayNearby 2026) instead of unsourced claims.
3. **DPDP with actual dates** (13 Nov 2025 / 13 Nov 2026 / 13 May 2027) and a plain-English checklist. No ranking page does this.
4. **A 10-task priority table, an ROI check with maths, and a week-by-week 30-day plan.**
5. **E-E-A-T and structure:** named founder byline, key takeaways, TOC, 8-question FAQ with schema, sources list, credits to Nexolve for cost ranges.
6. **Freshness:** reflects Meta's 1 Oct 2026 pricing changes and the 31 Dec 2026 INR billing deadline.
