Notes:
- All prices are **monthly except websites, which are priced per project** (owner, 19 Nov 2026).
- The owner wrote "Our charges starts from INR 25000" without naming the service. Read as SEO retainers (19 Nov 2026), since social media was given separately at ₹20,000; if the owner corrects this, update here.
- The owner did not say whether prices include GST. Don't state "plus GST" or "including GST" for Amatchless prices until confirmed.
- Always pair a price with "the exact scope and price come after a free discovery call".
