# Cover log: one unique concept per post

Every post gets its **own cover concept**. Before designing a new cover, read this log and pick a background colour, layout and main motif that **all differ** from the last three covers. Add a row when the post goes live.

Fixed for every cover (brand and crop rules): 1600x1000 JPG, brand palette and fonts, thick ink borders and hard shadows, the amatchless logo, and all text and the logo inside the centre 960px (x 320 to 1280) because the /blog/ card crops the sides. Decoration may sit near the edges.

| Week | Post | Background | Layout | Main motif |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | AI automation for small business India (new cover 18 Nov) | Ink (dark) with dot grid | Eyebrow + headline, then a horizontal flow row | 4-step automation flow (enquiry → reply → booked → invoice) |
| 2 | Influencer marketing cost in India (new cover 18 Nov) | Pink | Pill + question headline, then a row of tilted cards | Creator tier price cards (Nano to Celeb), hearts |
| 3 | How to get more Google reviews | Purple with a street-map pattern | Headline on top, a big product mock in the middle | Google Maps search result list ("near me") with star ratings, map pins |

## Ideas not used yet

- Giant number or rupee figure as the hero (cost and budget posts).
- **Mint background**, split layout: "before" vs "after" cards (comparison posts).
- A big phone or laptop mock with the topic's real UI (ads, ChatGPT, website posts).
- **White background with a thick coloured frame**, one oversized icon or sticker cluster (trends and planning posts).
- **Photo-style collage** of cut-out cards (case-study-led posts).
- A calendar or checklist object as the hero (planning posts).
