| Chatmeter, Trustmary, Victorious, WordStream-type US posts | US vendors | US-only, no WhatsApp, no Indian law |

**Our post (~3,800 words) wins on:**
1. Google's own words: ranking factors (support.google.com/business/answer/7091) and the full Maps content policy (contributionpolicy/answer/7400114), including the 2026 clauses on staff quotas, naming staff and on-premises pressure, which no India page explains.
2. Copy-paste WhatsApp templates in English, Hinglish and Hindi, plus SMS (with DLT note) and a reminder.
3. Timing table by business type (salon, clinic, restaurant, coaching, retail, home services, D2C).
4. Reply templates for every review type, including clinic privacy and suspected fakes.
5. Reporting and appeal steps (Reviews Management Tool), extortion advice, and Indian law (IS 19000:2022).
6. 30-day plan, 5 on-brand graphics plus a crop-safe cover, 8 FAQs, clickable sources.

Research note: the cloud network blocked most sites, so Google's help pages and BrightLocal's 2026 survey were read in full through the browser on the owner's computer. Ranking pages are from search results, not full reads.
