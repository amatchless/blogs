## 5. Tone and voice

- Direct, confident, plain-English, no jargon ("Clear reporting, straight talk, and no jargon to hide behind").
- Short punchy headlines, often two-beat: "Real brands. Real results." "Say hi. We'll take it from here."
- Warm and playful: cartoon team ("the matchless four": Bravo, Sales; Blossom, Creative; Dexter, Technology; Kim, Client Head); case studies told as stories ("Meanwhile, in Dexter's lab…").
- Practical and numbers-led: break-even maths, ROAS, hours saved.
- British/Indian spelling in places ("recognisable", "optimises"). Pick one standard for the blog.
- Existing posts open with a reframing hook ("Most small businesses don't have a marketing problem. They have a repetition problem.") and close with a soft, honest CTA.

## 8. SEO observations and gaps

**Technical / on-page**
- No meta description on /services/ or on any blog post (only og tags).
- Weak titles: "Services - Amatchless", blog posts "... - Amatchless".
- Blog title has a double-encoded "&amp;".
- /contact/ missing from the sitemap; /sample-page/ (WordPress default) is in the sitemap and errors. Remove it.
- No structured data visible (Organization, LocalBusiness, Article, FAQ, Review).
- Services footer says Gurugram but the confirmed location is Rohini, New Delhi; fix it for local SEO consistency; no street address or Google Business Profile signal.
- Contact links mix /contact/ and #contact.

**Content / authority**
- Only 3 blog posts, same date, despite "New articles every week".
- No named author or founder, cartoon team only: weak E-E-A-T.
- Blog posts don't link to /services/, /ai-automation/ or the matching case study (e.g. ROAS post should link to Bailey's Blossoms).
- No standalone service pages for SEO, Google Ads, Meta Ads, influencer marketing, branding, web/app development, e-commerce, booking systems. Blog posts have nothing specific to point to.
- No location pages (e.g. Delhi NCR, India, US, Canada) despite serving those markets.
- Case studies lack numbers except Bailey's Blossoms; AleuCo has no SEO metrics.
- Work page mentions "agri-tech" but no such case study.
- AI Automation meta mentions "sales, HR and onboarding" but the page never covers HR or onboarding.

## 9. Implications for the blog

1. Every post maps to one service and links to /services/ (or /ai-automation/) plus the closest case study, and ends with the discovery-call CTA.
2. Cover underserved pillars: SEO/AEO, Google Ads, influencer marketing, custom software, e-commerce for creators, booking systems.
3. Mix India-focused and US/Canada-focused topics to match the markets served.
4. Add a named author bio and Article schema; write a real meta description per post.
5. Recommended site fixes (outside the blog) that multiply blog SEO: individual service pages, a single confirmed address, Organization/LocalBusiness schema, sitemap cleanup.
